title: Account Deletion

import AndronixButton from "@/components/button";

If you no longer want to use Andronix, you can have your account and its data removed.

To request deletion, send an email to [support@andronix.app](mailto:support@andronix.app?subject=Account_Deletion), or use the button below. We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days of receiving your request.

<AndronixButton link={"mailto:support@andronix.app?subject=Account Deletion"}>
  Request Deletion
</AndronixButton>

### Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time, so we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes take effect immediately after they are posted on this page.
