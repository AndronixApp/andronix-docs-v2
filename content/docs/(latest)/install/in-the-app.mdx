<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Linux distros

On Home, under **Install Linux**, tap **Linux distros**. On a device that can run Linux in the app, the distro installs in the app; there's nothing to choose.

If you already used Andronix with Termux, check where new installs go: **Settings › Linux › Run Linux**. See [Coming from Termux](/install/standalone-and-termux#switch-between-them).

<AppShot name="app10-your-linux" alt="Home: Your Linux at the top, and Install Linux with the Linux distros card" />

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick a distro and a desktop

Choose the distro on **Distro Selection**. Then choose **Desktop Environment** or **Command line only (no desktop)**, and for a desktop, pick XFCE, LXQt, MATE or KDE Plasma. Not sure which? See [Choose a distro and desktop](/install/choose).

<Shots>
<AppShot name="app10-sa-pick-distro" alt="Distro Selection, step 1 of 4: Try out Modded OS, Ubuntu 26.04 LTS and Debian 13 (Recommended), Ubuntu 24.04 LTS" />
<AppShot name="app10-sa-pick-interface" alt="GUI Selection: Desktop Environment (Best) or Command line only (no desktop)" />
<AppShot name="app10-sa-pick-desktop" alt="Desktop Environments: XFCE (Recommended), LXQt, MATE and KDE Plasma (needs 4 GB of RAM or more)" />
</Shots>

- **Command line only (no desktop):** no desktop opens; the app opens a terminal where you use Linux. You can [add a desktop later](#add-a-desktop-later).
- **KDE Plasma** needs about 4 GB of RAM. On a device with less, the app warns you and suggests XFCE.

</Step>

<Step>

### Set up your user

Enter a **User name** (lower-case letters and numbers, for example `alex`) and a **Password** (at least 4 characters), then tap **Continue**. The root account stays locked; use `sudo` with this password for admin work. (On Void, `sudo` doesn't ask for a password; on Manjaro, use the root terminal for admin work.)

<AppShot name="app10-sa-setup-user" alt="Set up your user: User name and Password, with the note about sudo, and Continue" />

The app also passes your phone's time zone and keyboard layout to the distro.

</Step>

<Step>

### Wait for the install

The app shows each step as it goes: checking your device, downloading and unpacking the distro, installing the desktop and a web browser, and setting up your user. It usually takes **5 to 30 minutes**, depending on your phone and your connection.

<Shots>
<AppShot name="app10-sa-install-progress-download" alt="Installing Debian: downloading, with the bytes done and the Steps list" />
<AppShot name="app10-sa-install-progress" alt="Installing Debian: a later step, with the Steps list showing Done steps and their times" />
</Shots>

The install keeps going if you leave the app. Android shows an **Installing Debian** notification (with your distro's name) with the step, the time left and **Cancel**; tap it to come back. The first time, on Android 13 and newer, the app asks to send notifications so it can tell you when the install finishes.

To stop the install, tap **Cancel** (or press Back), then **Stop**. You can start it again later.

</Step>

<Step>

### Open the desktop

When the install is done, the app says **Debian is ready** (with your distro's name). Tap **Open the desktop**. For a command-line install, tap **Open the terminal** instead.

<AppShot name="app10-sa-install-done" alt="Debian is ready, with Open the desktop" />

A loading screen shows the steps (**Starting Linux**, **Starting the desktop**, **Almost ready**) and stays up until the desktop has really drawn. The first start can take a minute; later starts take a few seconds.

## Later starts

Your distro is listed on Home under **Your Linux**. Tap its row to open the desktop. While it runs, the row shows **Running** and an **Open** pill, with **Stop** and a **⋮** menu at the end.

<Shot name="standalone/your-linux" alt="Home, Your Linux: Debian running with an Open pill, Kali Modded and Void below" />

- **Leave the desktop:** press Back. Linux keeps running in the background with the notification; open it again from Home or the notification.
- **Stop it:** tap **Stop** (on the toolbar, on Home or in the notification). Apps on the desktop close and anything unsaved is lost.
- **One at a time:** one distro runs at a time. Opening another one asks you to stop the running one first.
- **Terminal:** the terminal button opens Andronix's own terminal for that distro. Command-line installs (without a desktop) open there.

## If the install fails

The app says what went wrong and what to try, for example **Not enough space** or **No connection**, with **Try again** and **Report a problem**. If you're not in the app, a notification says the install didn't finish, with **Try again** and **Send report**. See [Linux in the app: limits and problems](/troubleshooting/in-the-app).

<AppShot name="app10-sa-install-failed" alt="Debian didn't install: the reason, What to try, and the Report a problem and Try again buttons" />

## Add a desktop later

Installed a distro with **Command line only**? On Home, under **Your Linux**, tap **⋮** next to it, then **Add a desktop**, and pick XFCE, LXQt, MATE or KDE Plasma. Your files and apps stay; the distro stops while the desktop is added. Modded OS editions already have their desktop.

<AppShot name="app10-sa-add-desktop" alt="Add a desktop to Debian: your files and apps stay, with XFCE, LXQt, MATE and KDE Plasma" />
