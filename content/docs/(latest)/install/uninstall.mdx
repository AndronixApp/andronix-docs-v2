---
title: Uninstall a Distro
description: Remove a Linux distro you installed in the Andronix app, and what stays on your phone.
---

For a distro listed on Home under **Your Linux**:

1. On Home, under **Your Linux**, tap **⋮** next to the distro, then **Remove** (for example **Remove Debian 13…**).

   <AppShot name="app10-your-linux-menu" alt="The ⋮ menu for a distro under Your Linux, with Remove at the bottom" />

2. The app asks **Remove Debian?** (with your distro's name): the distro and everything inside it is deleted from this device. Tap **Remove**.

   <AppShot name="app10-remove-confirm" alt="Remove Debian? Debian and everything inside it is deleted from this device. Cancel and Remove" />

3. The app stops the distro if it's running, removes it, and says when it's done.

What stays: files you saved to **Download/Andronix** or **Documents/Andronix** stay on your phone. Home-screen shortcuts to the distro's apps stop working, and Android can't remove them for you: remove them from your home screen yourself. If you install the same distro again, they work again.

If the app says **Wait for the install to finish first**, an install is still running. If it says the distro **couldn't be removed**, close Andronix, open it again and try once more.

**Install a free distro again from scratch:** remove it, then install it again. Installing a distro that's already there changes nothing. A Modded OS edition can be reinstalled directly; see [Modded OS](/modded-os/modded-os-installation).

For a distro you installed in Termux, see [Uninstall a distro in Termux](/termux/uninstall).
