## About Andronix

Andronix runs a full Linux distro, like Debian, Ubuntu or Fedora, on your Android phone or tablet, with a real desktop and no root. New here? Follow the four steps below, in order.

<PlayLink />

<Callout type="info">**Andronix 2.0:** free distros install with one command, with the latest versions (Debian 13, Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, Fedora 44 and more), a choice of XFCE, LXQt, MATE or KDE Plasma, Firefox included, and a full-screen desktop with Termux:X11. [See what's supported](/reference/distros).</Callout>

<Callout type="info">**Using Andronix app 8.x or older?** These docs are for app 10 (Andronix 2.0). The docs for the old app, with its installer, VNC setup and Andronix Commands, are under [App 8.x (Classic)](/v8), also in the version menu at the top of the sidebar. Or update to app 10 and follow the steps here. Had a Modded OS on the old app? See [Upgrading from an older Modded OS](/modded-os/upgrade).</Callout>
