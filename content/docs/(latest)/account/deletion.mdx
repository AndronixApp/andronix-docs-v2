You can delete your Andronix account and its data at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and the little we have to keep.

## How to delete your account

**In the app (Andronix 10.0 or newer):** open your account and choose **Delete account**. For safety, the app asks you to sign in again first. Your data is deleted right away.

**By email:** if you can't use the app, email [support@andronix.app](mailto:support@andronix.app?subject=Account%20Deletion) from the address you signed up with. We delete your account and its data within 30 days of your request.

<Callout type="warning">Deleting your account can't be undone. Distros already installed on your phone aren't affected; they live in Termux, not in your account.</Callout>

## What we delete

- **Your sign-in account** (your email and login).
- **Your profile**, including saved commands and the Premium status from older versions of the app.
- **Your entry on the supporter wall**, if you had one.
- **Your purchases as linked to your account:** your Premium and Modded OS access, including access to the Classic Modded editions.
- **Donation records** linked to your account.
- **Download counters** we keep per account to apply the Modded download limits.
- **Your verified Google Play purchase records** as linked to your account.
- **Your app analytics profile:** it's queued for deletion from our self-hosted analytics database.

## What we keep, and why

Tax and legal rules require us to keep a record of sales. For each order you made, we keep **one purchase record**:

- the order ID, the product, the purchase time, the price, currency and country;
- **not** your email, name or account ID. The record is linked only to a one-way code that can't be turned back into your account.

These records are deleted automatically after **8 years**.

We also keep:

- **Google Play purchase tokens**, with your account ID replaced by the same one-way code. This stops a refunded or reused purchase from being claimed twice. If you make a new account later, you can restore a Google Play purchase to it by proving the purchase again (restoring purchases in the app).
- **A note that a deletion happened**, with the same one-way code and how many records were removed, so we can show the deletion was done.

## What isn't linked to your account

- **Installer telemetry** (from the `andronix` command) uses a random install ID, not your account, so there's nothing to delete. You can turn it off with `andronix telemetry off`.
- **The documentation site** (this site) uses cookie-free analytics that don't identify you.

For the full picture, read our [Privacy Policy](https://andronix.app/legal/privacy-policy).
