## Open and stop

- **Open the desktop:** tap the distro's row. The first start can take a minute; a loading screen shows **Starting Linux**, **Starting the desktop** and **Almost ready**.
- **Terminal:** tap **Terminal** at the end of the row to open Andronix's own terminal for that distro. While the distro runs, the row shows **Running** and an **Open** pill, and the terminal moves into the **⋮** menu (**Open the terminal**).
- **Command-line distros** (installed without a desktop) open in the terminal.
- **Stop:** tap **Stop** and confirm. Apps on the desktop close and anything unsaved is lost.

**One distro runs at a time.** To open a different one, stop the one that's running first.

On a tablet or an unfolded foldable, Home shows the distro's details next to the list. See [Tablets and foldables](/desktop/tablets).

## The ⋮ menu

Tap **⋮** next to a distro:

<AppShot name="app10-your-linux-menu" alt="The ⋮ menu: Add an app to the home screen, Start in safe mode, Desktop size, Add a desktop and Remove" />

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add an app to the home screen** | Pin a Linux app to Android's home screen (Android 8 and newer). See [Linux apps on your home screen](/use/shortcuts). |
| **Start in safe mode** / **Turn off safe mode** | Starts the desktop with lighter, safer settings, every time until you turn it off. See [Black screen and safe mode](/troubleshooting/black-screen). |
| **Desktop size** | **Standard**, **Sharp** or **Native**. See [Desktop size](/desktop/tablets#desktop-size). |
| **Add a desktop** | Only for command-line distros: adds XFCE, LXQt, MATE or KDE Plasma. Your files and apps stay. See [Add a desktop](/install/in-the-app#add-a-desktop-later). |
| **Remove** | Deletes the distro and everything inside it. See [Remove a distro in the app](/install/uninstall#in-the-app). |

## The Linux is running notification

While a distro runs, Android shows a notification with how long it has run and about how much memory it uses. Tap it to open the desktop.

<AppShot name="app10-linux-running-notification" alt="The notification: Debian · desktop running, the uptime and RAM, with Open terminal, Stop and Mute" />

Its buttons are **Open terminal** and **Stop**, plus **Mute** when the desktop has sound, or **Open desktop** when it doesn't. **Stop** in the notification stops the desktop, the terminal and any install right away, without asking, so save your work first.

## Keep Linux running

If battery optimisation applies to Andronix, the app shows **Keep Linux running** once. Tap it to turn battery optimisation off for Andronix, so Android doesn't stop Linux in the background.

If Android stops Linux anyway, you get **Android stopped Debian** (with your distro's name). Tap it to start it again, or **Why?** to read about Android's background limits: [Process completed (signal 9)](/troubleshooting/signal-9). The app keeps a log of what happened and attaches it to your next problem report.
