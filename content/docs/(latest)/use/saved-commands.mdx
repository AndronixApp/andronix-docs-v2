---
title: Your Saved Andronix Commands
description: Andronix Commands is retiring. View the commands you saved, save them as a file or share them, from Settings › Saved commands.
---

Andronix Commands, the old app's place to save your own Linux commands, is retiring. The commands you saved are still in your account, and the app shows them so you can keep a copy.

## Open your saved commands

Open **Settings** and, under **Tools**, tap **Saved commands**. The row shows only when your account has saved commands.

The sheet says how many commands you have and lists them. Nothing is deleted from your account now.

## Keep a copy

- **Save as file:** pick where to save it. The app suggests the name `andronix-commands.sh`. When it's saved, the app says **Commands saved.**
- **Share:** send the commands to another app, like your email or a notes app.

You can open **Saved commands** any time, even if you dismissed the notice about Commands on Home before.

## If they don't load

If the app says **Couldn't load your saved commands**, check your connection and tap **Try again**.
