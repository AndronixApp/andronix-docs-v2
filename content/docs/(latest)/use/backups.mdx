---
title: Back Up Your Files
description: Keep your files from a distro in the app - there's no in-app backup yet, so copy them out with the Files app or the shared folders - and move a Termux backup into the app.
---

import NextStep from "@/components/next-step";

There's no backup for distros in the app yet. To keep your files, copy them out: your Linux home shows in Android's **Files** app under **Andronix**, and you can save files to `~/Android/Download` or `~/Android/Documents` in Linux, which appear in **Download/Andronix** and **Documents/Andronix** on your phone. See [Android files in Linux](/use/android-files).

You can bring a backup from Termux **into** the app: see [Move a distro from Termux into the app](/install/standalone-and-termux#move-a-distro-from-termux-into-the-app).

For distros in Termux, `andronix backup` saves the whole distro: see [Back up and restore in Termux](/termux/backups).
