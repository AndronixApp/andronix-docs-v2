Sound is built in: the desktop's sound plays through the phone's speakers or headphones. It starts with the app's desktop, keeps playing when you leave the app, and ends when you stop Linux. **Mute** is on the desktop's toolbar and in the **Linux is running** notification. There's no microphone yet.

## No sound?

- **Check Mute** on the toolbar and in the notification, and the phone's media volume.
- **Another app is playing sound:** Linux pauses while another app has the audio. Close or pause it.
- **Sound stopped after you left and reopened the app:** stop the desktop, then open it again.
- **A command-line install** has no desktop, so it has no sound.
- **VNC from a computer:** sound plays on the phone, not the computer, and only while the app's desktop is running. See [Use the desktop from a computer](/desktop/in-the-app#use-the-desktop-from-a-computer-vnc).
- **Still nothing?** [Report a problem](/troubleshooting/report-a-problem) from the app.

In Termux, sound works differently: see [Sound in Termux](/termux/sound).
