title: Sound
description: Sound from your Andronix distro plays in the app, through the phone's speakers or headphones, with nothing to set up.
---

import NextStep from "@/components/next-step";

Sound is built in: the desktop's sound plays in the Andronix app, and keeps playing when you leave the app. **Mute** is on the desktop's toolbar and in the **Linux is running** notification. There's no microphone yet.

In Termux, sound works differently: see [Sound in Termux](/termux/sound).
