## Add an app

1. On Home, under **Your Linux**, tap **⋮** next to your distro, then **Add an app to the home screen**.
2. Pick the app. The list shows the distro's apps with their icons, with search at the top; the distro's settings dialogs are grouped under **Desktop settings**. **Terminal** opens Andronix's own terminal instead of the desktop.
3. Android asks where to put it. Confirm, and the app's icon appears on your home screen.

**Tapping the icon** starts the distro's desktop if it isn't running, opens the desktop and starts the app. If another distro is running, the app asks to stop it first.

If your home screen can't add icons from apps, Andronix adds the shortcut to its own icon instead: touch and hold the Andronix icon to open it from there.

## When you remove a distro

Android doesn't let apps remove icons from your home screen, so the shortcuts stay but stop working: tapping one says "This Linux was removed from Andronix." If you install the same distro again, they work again. To get rid of one, drag it off your home screen.

To remove a distro: on Home, tap **⋮** next to it, then **Remove**. The distro and everything inside it are deleted from this device.
