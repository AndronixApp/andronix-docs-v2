What changed in each release of Andronix: the `andronix` installer, the free distros, Modded OS and the app. For the story behind 2.0, read the blog post [Andronix 2.0](https://andronix.app/blog/andronix-2-0).

## Andronix 2.0

Installer 2.0.0 and app 10.0 · <ReleaseDate />

### Installer

- New installer, `andronix` 2.0.0, published at `dl.andronix.app`. One command installs a distro; no app needed ([Install without the app](/install/without-the-app)).
- Works with Termux from F-Droid, GitHub and Google Play (Android 11 and newer for Play).
- Installs `proot` itself, repairs a half-upgraded Termux, and checks the checksum of every download.
- New commands:
  - `andronix update` updates the installer and your distros ([Keep it updated](/use/updates)).
  - `andronix backup` backs up a distro to phone storage ([Back up and restore](/use/backups)).
  - `andronix pack add python|node|java|go|db` adds dev packs; PostgreSQL works under proot ([Dev packs](/use/dev-packs)).
  - `andronix tune` switches to a lighter performance profile ([Make it faster](/use/performance)).
- Optional, cookie-free telemetry, with `andronix telemetry off` to turn it off.

### Free distros

- Nine distros on current versions: Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, Ubuntu 24.04 LTS, Debian 13, Kali rolling, Fedora 44, Arch Linux ARM, Manjaro ARM, Alpine 3.24 and Void ([Supported distros](/reference/distros)).
- Desktops: XFCE, LXQt, MATE and KDE Plasma. KDE Plasma is on Ubuntu, Debian and Kali, and needs 4 GB of RAM.
- Firefox is included.
- A normal user account is created at install. You no longer run as root.
- Window managers are retired. Old window-manager installs map to XFCE.

### Desktop

- The desktop opens full screen with Termux:X11: `andronix desktop <distro>` ([Start the desktop](/desktop/termux-x11)). Termux:X11 comes from GitHub (the nightly release, universal-debug APK).
- Sound works out of the box: PulseAudio is set up automatically ([Sound](/use/sound)).
- VNC is still available ([Other ways to connect](/reference/vnc)).

### Modded OS

- Modded OS 2.0: five editions (Ubuntu 26.04 XFCE, Ubuntu 26.04 KDE with Plasma 6, Debian 13 XFCE, Manjaro XFCE and Kali XFCE), with one Andronix look and updates included.
- Classic editions: the original Modded Ubuntu XFCE, Ubuntu KDE, Debian and Manjaro, with their original look, rebuilt on current systems. Not sold; in the app under "Archived — yours to keep", free for everyone who bought them.
- Fixed: the Modded download outage (June to September 2026).
- Past buyers: purchases are verified with Google Play and kept on our servers, and Classic editions stay free. Owning Classic editions doesn't give a discount on Modded 2.0.

### Premium

- $2.99: Modded Pass 30% off\*, early access to new Modded editions and beta installers, higher Modded download limits, and an optional spot on the supporter wall. Existing Premium owners keep everything.

### App 10.0

- New dark design and new icon.
- Simpler install flow: the Termux step shows the command.
- A notification with the install result.
- A one-tap "Start the desktop" step with Termux:X11.
- Account deletion in the app.
- Optional, cookie-free analytics (OpenPanel, self-hosted), with an opt-out in Settings. No emails or names.
- Retired: Andronix Commands (export your saved commands from the app). Removed: Andronix Auto.

### Website and docs

- New website and docs. The docs follow a step-by-step path (Step 1 to Step 4), and every old docs link redirects to its new page.

### Known issues

- On some Android 9 phones with a 4.4 kernel, desktops may show a black screen. The command line works, and the installer warns you. See [The installer warns about an old kernel](/troubleshooting/install#the-installer-warns-about-an-old-kernel).
