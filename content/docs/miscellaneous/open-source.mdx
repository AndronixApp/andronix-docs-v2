This page explains which parts of Andronix are open source and how open-source developers can get everything for free.

## Is Andronix open source? 📖🔓

Yes and no: Andronix is partially open source. The free distro (.tar) files and the shell scripts are available on
our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/andronixapp). The paid parts, like the Android app itself and all
the files for Andronix Modded OS, are closed source for obvious reasons.

That doesn't mean we don't love open source. **We** ❤ **open source**! If you're a developer or maintainer of an
open-source project, **we will be more than happy to provide you everything for free** for life. Just get in touch with
us and complete the verification process 😊.

<Callout type={"info"}>
    Please email us your GitHub username and the name of the project you maintain at <a
    href="mailto:support@andronix.app">support@andronix.app</a>, and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
</Callout>
